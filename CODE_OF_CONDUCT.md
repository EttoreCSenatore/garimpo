# Code of Conduct

Garimpo follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome people of every background and experience level. Give and accept feedback graciously, focus on what is best for the project and the community, and own your mistakes.

Not acceptable: harassment or discrimination of any kind, insults or personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, publishing someone's private information, trolling, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge harmful.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/EttoreCSenatore/garimpo/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct report) or contact the maintainer, [@EttoreCSenatore](https://github.com/EttoreCSenatore), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and fairly.
